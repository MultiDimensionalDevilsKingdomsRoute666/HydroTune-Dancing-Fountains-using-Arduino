# HydroTune: Dancing-Fountains-using-Arduino
An Arduino based project where audio input is analyzed and split into 7 frequency bands. The frequency bands are encoded into 6 DC water pumps, and the water pumps are activated depending on the amplitude of the corresponding frequency band. 
![Circuit Diagram](https://github.com/YazanRihan/Dancing-Fountains-using-Arduino/blob/c13a717598edc4dfb72969e9d86dc48fff4c4334/Circuit-Diagram.jpg)


## Summary

A fork of [YazanRihan/HydroTune-Dancing-Fountains-using-Arduino](https://github.com/YazanRihan/HydroTune-Dancing-Fountains-using-Arduino). An Arduino water fountain that dances to music by mapping audio frequency bands to pumps.

## What's inside

`code.ino` (the sketch), `AudioAnalyzer.cpp` and `.h` (the audio analysis) and the circuit diagram (`.fzz` and `.jpg`).

## How to view or run it

Wire the circuit from the diagram, then open `code.ino` in the Arduino IDE and upload it.
